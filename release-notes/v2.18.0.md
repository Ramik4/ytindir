## ✨ New Features

- Subtitles: download a video's subtitles in the languages you choose (your phone's language by default), with YouTube's automatic translation when a language has no subtitles of its own; saved in the video, as a separate .srt file, or both (Settings → Processing → Video, and the Subtitles chip in the download card)
- File name templates: the title alone is now the default name; ready-made templates (Title, Channel - Title, Title [ID], Title - Channel, Title - Date) with an example of the name they give
- A subfolder per channel (Settings → Folders → Save to subdirectory)

## 🔧 Changes

- YouTube quick download list: "All formats" is now "All options" and opens the download card with all its settings; there the format field opens the full format list
- Link details load faster: YouTube's player is no longer downloaded and solved again for every link
- Downloads keep going on slow or unstable networks instead of stopping
- Videos in parts (Instagram, X, TikTok and others) download faster, 4 parts at a time
- A subtitle that can't be downloaded no longer stops the video's download
- "Adjust video" in the download card in two rows: Thumbnail, Audio, Subtitles / Cut, Crop, Filename template
- Videos downloaded from the Image tab are saved in the Video folder; pictures and GIFs stay in Images
