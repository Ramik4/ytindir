## 🔧 Changes and improvements

- "All options" in the quick download menu of YouTube RLX and YT Music RLX opens the download card with all its settings right away, instead of YTindir's quick list; there the format field opens the full format list
- The download card opens whole on the screen in every tab, down to the link at the bottom, without pulling it up
- "Adjust audio" in the download card in two rows: Thumbnail, Lyrics, Bitrate / Cut, Filename template
