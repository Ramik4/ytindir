## ✨ New Features

- New Image tab: downloads every image of a post (a whole carousel or gallery) from Instagram, X (Twitter), Pinterest, Reddit, Tumblr and many more sites, with gallery-dl
- gallery-dl comes with the app and updates itself, with its own screen in Settings → Updating
- GIFs come as real .gif files (X, Pinterest, Reddit)
- Instagram posts download without an account too
- Reddit images and videos download again, without an account (videos up to 1080p, with sound)
- Images are saved in Download/YTindir/Images and show in Downloads and the gallery, named username_postid_1, _2…

## 🔧 Changes

- A post without a video, or one yt-dlp can't read, opens the Image tab instead of an error
- Clearer error messages for Instagram and Reddit
- Bug fixes and other improvements
