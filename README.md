YTindir downloads audio, video and images from YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, VK, X, Reddit, Pinterest and more than 1000 other websites. It is built on yt-dlp and YTDLnis, with a simpler interface, a refreshed look and a setup that keeps itself working when websites change.

## 🎬 Download quality

| Sources | Supported resolutions |
| --- | --- |
| **YouTube** | 8K 60fps, 4K 60fps, 2K 60fps, 1080p FHD 60fps and other resolutions |
| **Instagram** | 2K 60fps, 1080p FHD 60fps (posts, reels, stories) and other resolutions |
| **TikTok** | 1080p FHD 60fps (in source quality) and other resolutions |
| **X (Twitter)** | 4K 60fps, 2K 60fps, 1080p FHD 60fps and other resolutions |
| **Facebook** | 2K 60fps, 1080p FHD 60fps and other resolutions |
| **VK** | 2K 60fps, 1080p FHD 60fps and other resolutions |
| **Reddit** | 1080p FHD (with sound), images and GIFs without an account |
| **Other sites** | The highest supported resolutions the site offers |

## ✨ Features

- ⚡ **Quick download list for YouTube**: opens as soon as you share a link, with each quality's badge (8K, 4K, 2K, FHD, HD), frame rate, codec and real file size; pick one and tap **Download**. Also M4A (source audio), MP3 192 kbps and the original thumbnail
- 📋 **All options** when you want full control: the download card with all its settings, and the full list of every video and audio track, original and dubbed audio grouped apart
- 📤 **Download from the share menu** of any app, or straight from the download button of **YouTube RLX** and **YT Music RLX**: their quick download menu shows the qualities with file sizes and queues the download in YTindir, and its **All options** opens YTindir's download card
- ✂️ **Trim and crop**: download only part of a video, or crop it (for example to a square)
- 🖼️ **Image tab**: every image of a post (a whole carousel or gallery) from Instagram, X, Pinterest, Reddit, Tumblr and more, with gallery-dl; GIFs as real .gif files
- 🔤 **Subtitles** in the languages you choose (your phone's language by default), with YouTube's automatic translation when a language has none; inside the video, as an .srt file or both
- 📁 **File name templates** (the title alone by default) and a **subfolder per channel**
- 🎞️ **Compatible video**: VP9, AV1 and HEVC from social media converted to H.264 at near-original quality, for players and editors that need it
- 🔁 **Self-repair**: yt-dlp updates itself; if a download fails after a website change, YTindir updates yt-dlp and retries on its own. Downloads keep going on slow or unstable networks, and videos in parts download 4 parts at a time
- 💬 **Clear errors**: each error explains what went wrong and what to do, with one-tap fixes such as retrying without the certificate check
- 🎤 **Synced lyrics** embedded into audio files, **SponsorBlock**, clean file names and short names like `username_postid` for social media
- 🍪 **Cookies**, **PO token**, proxy and IPv4 options for content that is hard to download
- 🔄 **In-app updates**, backup and restore, and a terminal for yt-dlp commands
- 🎨 **YTindir** or **Material You** colours, light and dark themes, pure black for AMOLED screens and a frosted glass navigation bar
- 🌍 **12 languages**: English, Turkish, Russian, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), German, French, Arabic, Indonesian, Chinese (Simplified), Japanese and Korean

## 📥 Installation

Download the APK from **[Releases](https://github.com/RAMiK4/ytindir/releases/latest)**: **arm64** for most phones, **universal** for every device. YTindir installs easily, without clashing with other apps, and new versions install over the old one without losing data.
